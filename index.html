How it is made

Terrain: a 1024² fBm + ridged-noise heightfield in a mountain-ringed valley, chunked for culling and splatted in the shader (grass, dirt, rock by slope, pond bank, cobble, snow) with two-scale sampling against tiling. Grass: up to 700k GPU-instanced blades that wrap around the player, read height and density from textures, bend in the wind and part around your feet. Trees: alpha-tested leaf cards with crown-spherical normals and sharpened alpha edges. Pond: three.js planar-reflection water with depth-based colour, transparency and shoreline foam. Sky: Preetham sky with PMREM image-based lighting, procedural clouds, stars and a moon. People: flat black-to-transparent silhouettes (four outlines) that follow the road network as camera-facing billboards with a sheared walk cycle. Post: HDR, MSAA, ground-truth ambient occlusion, screen-space sun shafts, bloom, ACES tone mapping and grading; resolution adapts to keep the frame rate up. Technique references: Codrops fluffy grass, al-ro grass, the three.js showcase forum.
